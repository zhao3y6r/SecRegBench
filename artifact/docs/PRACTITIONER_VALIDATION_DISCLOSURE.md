# Practitioner validation disclosure (v0.14)

The authors report that three securities compliance practitioners reviewed the
benchmark design, rule interpretations, action-label logic, and corpus examples
and found no material compliance-label defect requiring correction. The paper
describes them only as three anonymous securities compliance practitioners.

Their identities, employers, and raw comments are intentionally excluded from
the anonymous submission and public artifact. This disclosure records an
author-supplied attestation; it is not a substitute for an item-level public
annotation ledger, inter-annotator agreement statistic, or legal certification.

The benchmark remains a research diagnostic. Practitioner review does not
authorize automated investment advice, production compliance certification, or
legal reliance.
