# Practitioner validation disclosure

Five anonymous securities compliance practitioners reviewed 500 frozen
SecRegBench items. The deterministic assignment contained 50 common items
reviewed by all five and 450 disjoint items reviewed once, yielding 700
judgments.

Each practitioner selected one customer-visible action, rated realism from 1
to 5, and marked whether the scenario had a material defect. Shared-item
action pairwise agreement was 100% and Fleiss kappa was 1.0. Across all 500
items, practitioner actions matched the rule-compiled primary action on 77.8%,
mean realism was 4.01/5, and 7 items (1.4%; Wilson 95% CI 0.7--2.9%) were
marked as materially defective.

The five-way agreement estimate applies only to the 50 shared items; the other
450 items have one judgment each. The seven flags remain in the release. Three
occur in held-out, and excluding them changes any released accuracy, macro-F1,
or unsafe-fulfilment point estimate by at most 0.074 percentage points.
SecRegBench describes its labels as rule-compiled policy targets, not legal
certification or production approval.

The public files contain only anonymous reviewer slots and pseudonymous item
IDs, with no practitioner names, employers, or contact information.
