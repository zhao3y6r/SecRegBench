# Practitioner validation disclosure

Five anonymous securities compliance practitioners reviewed 500 frozen
SecRegBench items. The deterministic assignment contained 50 common items
reviewed by all five and 450 disjoint items reviewed once, yielding 700
judgments.

Each practitioner selected one customer-visible action, rated realism from 1
to 5, and marked whether the scenario had a material defect. Mean realism was
4.69/5; all five ratings on every shared item lay within one point. No item was
marked as having a material scenario defect. Shared-item action pairwise
agreement was 36.0% and Fleiss kappa was 0.063. Aggregate practitioner actions
matched the rule-compiled primary action on 36.0% of items.

The low action agreement does not validate a unique operational action label.
It indicates sensitivity to reviewer policy and to the compressed four-choice
rubric. SecRegBench therefore describes its labels as rule-compiled policy
targets, not expert gold, legal certification, or production approval.

The public files contain only anonymous reviewer slots and pseudonymous item
IDs, with no practitioner names, employers, or contact information.
