# SecRegBench five-practitioner validation

Status: `PASS_COMPLETED_FIVE_ANONYMOUS_SECURITIES_COMPLIANCE_PRACTITIONERS`

## Design and integrity

- Five anonymous practitioners.
- 500 distinct frozen items.
- 50 shared items reviewed by all five; 450 additional items reviewed once.
- 700/700 answer lines valid; all question text and state context unchanged.

## Main results

- Shared-item action pairwise agreement: 100.0% (95% CI 100.0--100.0%).
- Shared-item action Fleiss kappa: 1.000 (95% CI 1.000--1.000).
- Shared-item realism ICC(2,1): 0.491 (95% CI 0.142--0.721).
- Human aggregate versus machine-oracle primary action: 77.8% (95% CI 74.2--81.4%).
- Mean realism: 4.01/5 (95% CI 3.99--4.03).
- Material-defect rate: 1.4% (7/500; Wilson 95% CI 0.7--2.9%).

## Interpretation boundary

The study supports perceived realism and shows unanimous action choices on the 50 shared items. Across all 500 items, practitioner actions match the rule-compiled primary target on 77.8%, while seven items are marked as having a material scenario defect. The five-way agreement estimate applies only to the deliberately shared 50-item subset; the other 450 items have one practitioner judgment each. The study is not a legal opinion, production-safety certification, or proof that synthetic dialogue frequencies represent customer traffic.
