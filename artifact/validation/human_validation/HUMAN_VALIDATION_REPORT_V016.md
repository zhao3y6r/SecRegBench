# SecRegBench five-practitioner validation

Status: `PASS_COMPLETED_FIVE_ANONYMOUS_SECURITIES_COMPLIANCE_PRACTITIONERS`

## Design and integrity

- Five anonymous practitioners.
- 500 distinct frozen items.
- 50 shared items reviewed by all five; 450 additional items reviewed once.
- 700/700 answer lines valid; all question text and state context unchanged.

## Main results

- Shared-item action pairwise agreement: 36.0% (95% CI 32.0--40.4%).
- Shared-item action Fleiss kappa: 0.063 (95% CI 0.003--0.116).
- Shared-item realism ICC(2,1): 0.108 (95% CI 0.063--0.157).
- Human aggregate versus machine-oracle primary action: 36.0% (95% CI 31.8--40.2%).
- Mean realism: 4.69/5 (95% CI 4.65--4.73).
- Material-defect rate: 0.0% (0/500; Wilson 95% CI 0.0--0.8%).

## Interpretation boundary

The study supports high perceived realism and found no material scenario defect in the sampled items. Low action agreement does not validate a unique operational action label; instead, it exposes sensitivity to reviewer policy and to the compressed action rubric. The study is not a legal opinion, production-safety certification, or proof that synthetic dialogue frequencies represent customer traffic. Agreement is estimated only on the 50 shared items; the other 450 items have one practitioner judgment each.
