# Notices and disclaimers

## Synthetic benchmark

SecRegBench scenarios are synthetic research examples. They are not customer
communications, instructions issued by a securities firm, or descriptions of
specific real customers. Any resemblance to a particular person, account,
conversation, or case is unintended.

## Regulatory source material

The benchmark design is informed by public official sources. This repository
contains bibliographic metadata, official URLs, dates, locators,
machine-readable rule abstractions, and the 48 short attributed excerpts used
as evaluation evidence. It intentionally excludes raw source snapshots and
full official documents. Copyright and database rights in third-party source
documents, if any, remain with their respective rights holders.

## Model-related material

The repository contains parsed experimental outputs and aggregate generation
statistics, but no model implementation or weights. Names such as Qwen,
DeepSeek, and OpenAI are used only to identify tools or checkpoints involved in
the documented research. All corresponding trademarks belong to their
respective owners. No endorsement is implied.

## No professional advice or certification

The benchmark does not provide legal, compliance, or investment advice. A
benchmark score does not establish regulatory compliance, production safety,
fitness for customer use, or approval by any regulator, exchange, securities
firm, or model provider.

## Practitioner review

Five securities compliance practitioners are described anonymously. The
release includes deidentified item-level choices and aggregate statistics,
but their review is not a legal opinion, institutional approval, production
certification, or endorsement.
